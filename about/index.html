About us

Raised in Calgary rinks

Isaac and Beckham both came up through Glenlake and the Royals. They run Ascend together and coach every session themselves.

Photo: Bernard Spragg, CC0

I had coaches growing up who made hockey feel like something to survive. We started Ascend so no kid in Calgary has to go through that.

Isaac Matthews, co-founder

Ascend started with a simple idea. Kids should leave the rink better than they arrived and excited to come back. Too many programs run the same drills every coach already runs, with groups so big that a player gets a handful of reps and no real feedback.

We keep groups small, we teach from our own method and we coach the person as much as the player. Effort and character matter here as much as skill.

Meet the coaches

Isaac Matthews

Co-founder · Head Coach, Glenlake Hawks U15 BC1

Isaac played for Glenlake and the Royals growing up. Some of the coaching he had as a kid was bad enough that he built Ascend so no other player goes through it. He's head coach of the Glenlake Hawks U15 BC1 for 2026/27 and has also been an assistant coach with the Royals U15 AA. He's spent years helping players reach the highest level they're capable of.

BK Photo of Beckham coming soon

Beckham Keon

Co-founder · Associate Coach, Glenlake Hawks U15 BC1

Beckham also played for the Royals and Glenlake growing up. He coaches the whole player and believes kids improve faster when they're encouraged than when they're afraid of making mistakes. He's associate coach of the Glenlake Hawks U15 BC1 and has the same track record of moving players up to their highest level.

The Ascend standard

Effort, attitude and coachability are expected from every player. Talent gets developed here. Character is required.
