Questions parents ask us

Still not sure? Email ascendelitehockey@gmail.com and one of us will get back to you.

Photo: Bernard Spragg, CC0
What age groups do you coach?

Our programs are built for U9 to U15 players. The Rodeo Showcase also runs a U18 division.

How much attention will my player get?

Every session is planned so players get constant reps and personal feedback from Isaac and Beckham.

What should my player bring?

Full equipment, a water bottle with their name on it and sharpened skates.

What if we miss a session?

Email us before you register and we will walk you through our missed-session and refund policy.

How does payment work?

Once you register, we'll email you within 48 hours to confirm the spot and send payment details.

Which program is right for my player?

Tell us about your player in the notes on the registration form, or email us. We'll point you to the right fit before you pay anything.
