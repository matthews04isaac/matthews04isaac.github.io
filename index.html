Why Ascend exists

I had coaches growing up who made hockey feel like something to survive. We started Ascend so no kid in Calgary has to go through that.

Isaac Matthews, co-founder

Ascend is a Calgary hockey development program. Groups stay small so every player gets real reps and real feedback from a coach who knows their name, their game and what they need to work on next.

We train the whole player. Skating comes first because everything else in hockey is built on it, then puck skills, reads and compete. Kids leave better, and they leave wanting to come back.

Meet the coaches

The Ascend Method

Four stages. Every skill.

Every skill we teach moves through the same progression, from one clean mechanic to game speed.

01

Isolate

One mechanic at a time, no puck, no pressure.

02

Integrate

Connect the pieces into one movement, then add the puck.

03

Recognize

Learn the cues that tell a player which skill the moment needs.

04

Execute

Do it at game speed against real compete.
