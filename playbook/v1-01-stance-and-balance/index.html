Chapter 1

Stance and balance

Every skating skill starts from the stance. If a player can’t hold a balanced stance standing still, nothing built on top of it will hold up at speed.

What we look for

  • Knees bent so they sit over the toes
  • Weight over the balls of the feet, not the heels
  • Chest up and eyes up
  • Hands out in front, ready to hold a stick

How we isolate it

Players hold the stance on a glide across the ice with no stick and no puck. Coaches check one cue at a time and correct it before adding the next.
