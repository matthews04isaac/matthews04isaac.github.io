The Ascend Method

Four stages. Every skill.

Skating-first and shaped by Swedish development, where players master a movement before they're asked to use it at game speed.

Photo: Ron Cogswell, CC BY 2.0
01

Isolate

Break a skill down to one mechanic and own it. No puck, no pressure, full attention on how the body moves.

  • One cue at a time
  • Slow enough to feel it, then build speed
  • On the ice: push and full recovery on a straight line
02

Integrate

Connect the pieces into one movement, then add the puck once the movement holds up.

  • Link mechanics into full strides and turns
  • Add the puck without losing form
  • On the ice: crossover start into a carry
03

Recognize

Add reads. Players learn the cues that tell them which skill the moment needs.

  • Reading pressure, gaps and support
  • Choosing between two or three options
  • On the ice: read the checker's gap, pick the escape
04

Execute

Do it at game speed against real compete. This is where the work shows up in a Saturday game.

  • Full speed, live opponents
  • Small-area battles and finishing
  • On the ice: small-area battles with a live finish
